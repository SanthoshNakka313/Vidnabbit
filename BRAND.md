# VidNabbit Brand

VidNabbit is a fast, friendly media downloader for videos, images and audio. Paste a link, then preview it and save it in the format and quality you want.
Domain: vidnabbit.com (free at the time of naming, so register it before launch).

**Positioning:** A tool for saving media you own or have the right to download, such as your own uploads, Creative Commons or royalty-free media, and direct file links. Never market it as a way to rip copyrighted content or bypass DRM. App stores reject that.

## Logo

The mark is a rabbit (the "nabbit") whose face is a **play triangle pointing down onto a tray**. It reads as a play button and a download arrow at once.

## Typography

- Font: **Nunito** (Google Fonts, free for commercial use), weights 400 / 700 / 900
- Headings: Nunito 900, letter-spacing -0.5px
- Buttons, labels, file sizes: Nunito 700
- Body: Nunito 400, 16px base, line-height 1.6

## Voice

- Playful, quick and helpful, like a clever rabbit. Short sentences.
- Examples: "Paste a link. Nab it." · "Nabbed! Your video is ready." · "Hop to it: 3 files downloading."
- Microcopy can use rabbit puns sparingly (hop, nab, burrow). Errors and legal text stay plain and clear.

## UI style

- Rounded and friendly: cards 16px radius, buttons 12px, inputs 12px, pills fully rounded.
- One big paste-link input as the hero, with a gradient "Nab it" button.
- Results as cards: thumbnail, title, duration, then format/quality chips (MP4 1080p, MP4 720p, MP3, JPG).
- Soft shadows, generous white space, mobile-first.
- Download progress uses the brand gradient as the progress bar fill.

## Taglines
- **Primary:** "Paste it. Nab it."
- Alternates: "Grab media in a hop." · "Your media, nabbed fast."
