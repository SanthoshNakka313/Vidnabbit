VidNabbit is a fast, friendly downloader for videos, images and audio. The logo is a rabbit (the "nabbit") whose face is a play button pointing down onto a tray, so it reads as play and download at the same time.

Typography & voice

Paste it. Nab it.
Nunito 900 for headings, 700 for buttons, 400 for body text.
Keep the voice playful and quick: "Nabbed! Your video is ready." Errors and legal text stay plain and clear.

Do's and don'ts

  • ✅ Use the provided files. Use the dark version on dark backgrounds.
  • ✅ Position VidNabbit for media you own or have rights to.
  • ❌ Don't stretch, rotate or recolour the logo, and don't add shadows.
  • ❌ Don't use the horizontal logo below 120 px wide. Use the icon instead.
